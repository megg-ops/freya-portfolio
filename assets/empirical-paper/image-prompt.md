# 封面设计与生图提示词

## 设计方向

封面沿用网站“日光首屏 A”的纸张、水彩、铅笔、苔绿与陶土色，但不再使用树木、根系、植物生长或土层。整体采用日系动漫背景水彩：轻线稿、简化形体、清透色块与柔和水彩晕染，不使用真人或写实手部。画面应直接表达项目的责任边界与工作机制：

`原始数据 → 确定性审计 + 受限语义批注 → 用户确认 → 结构化数字真源 → 多重质量门禁 → 论文与复现材料`

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape cover illustration for the empirical-paper project detail page in a personal job-search portfolio
Primary request: visualize the actual mechanism of an AI-assisted empirical research workflow as a hand-crafted research apparatus / editorial cutaway, not a growth metaphor and not a software dashboard.

Show a warm paper-covered workbench with a clear left-to-right mechanism. Raw spreadsheet sheets enter a calibrated inspection station with ruler ticks, an alignment grid and a magnifying lens. Above it, a separate translucent vellum annotation lane adds semantic notes but cannot erase or rewrite the rigid factual rail. At their junction, use a small self-contained terracotta confirmation press with a simple lever and a round clay seal; show no person or body parts. Both lanes feed into one central locked evidence ledger containing aligned number chips, citation cards and figure/table slips. The ledger then sends a manuscript through three verification frames representing numeric consistency, document integrity and final quality. At the far right, output one polished paper plus a compact reproducibility bundle.

Style: Japanese anime watercolor background illustration, clean expressive pencil/ink contours, simplified shapes, restrained cel-shaded color blocks, translucent watercolor blooms and soft animation lighting on warm fibrous paper. Reduce realistic wood grain, metal reflections and photographic material detail.
Palette: warm paper #F7F4EC, pale sage #EEF3E4, parchment #E7DECB, moss green #5F8250, young-leaf green #8CAC69, terracotta #B8734A, charcoal #33372E and muted brass.
Composition: wide 16:10 landscape; mechanism fills the lower two-thirds; central ledger slightly right of center; generous uncluttered upper-left space for separate HTML title copy.
Text: no readable text, letters, numbers, pseudo-words or watermark.
Avoid: realistic hands, people or body parts, photorealistic antique materials, heavy sepia, trees, roots, branches, leaves, flowers, vines, seedlings, soil cross-sections, AI brains, robots, laptop heroes, dashboards, flowchart boxes, neon, blue-purple technology palettes, glassmorphism, 3D renders, corporate vector art and decorative gears.
```
